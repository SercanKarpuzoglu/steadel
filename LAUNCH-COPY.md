# Steadel — Launch copy kit

Ready-to-paste copy for the distribution work in [MARKETING.md](MARKETING.md)
(channels C3/C6 and asset task M4). Written in the **company-brand voice**:
calm, specific, no hype.

**One honesty rule, everywhere:** lead with what ships today — low-stock alerts
and scheduled reports. The **ads guard is Beta**; never headline it, always label
it. If a form has no room for the caveat, leave ads-guard out entirely.

## 4. YouTube (@Steadel_App)

Zero budget, so: screen recordings with a calm voice-over, no intro animation,
no music. **60–120 seconds each.** Pair each video with its guide — embed it on
the guide page and link the guide in the description.

**Every description ends with:**

> Steadel — low-stock alerts and scheduled reports for Shopify & WooCommerce.
> EU-hosted in Germany, GDPR-first. 14-day free trial, no card required:
> https://app.steadel.com

### Video 1 — "Get a low-stock alert before you sell out" (~90s)
*Pairs with:* `/guides/gdpr-low-stock-alerts`

1. **(0:00–0:10) The problem, on screen.** Show a product page with 3 units left.
   *"This is a bestseller. In about a day, it'll be gone — and nobody will notice
   until the refunds start."*
2. **(0:10–0:35) Connect.** Stores → paste store address → the one-click approval
   screen → connected. *"Read-only. It reads products and inventory, nothing
   else."*
3. **(0:35–0:60) Set a threshold.** Set it to 10, choose email.
4. **(0:60–0:85) The payoff.** Drop the stock, show the alert email arriving.
   *"That's the whole product: you find out while you can still do something."*
5. **(0:85–0:90) Close.** *"Hosted in Germany, no tracking. Free for 14 days."*

### Video 2 — "Shopify low-stock alerts in 3 minutes" (~90s)
*Pairs with:* `/guides/shopify-low-stock-alerts`

Same shape, Shopify path. Spend the extra seconds on the permissions screen —
being visibly read-only is the trust moment. End on the scheduled report email.

### Video 3 — "What we do with your data (and where it lives)" (~75s)
*Pairs with:* `/guides/gdpr-low-stock-alerts`

No product demo — this is the wedge, said plainly to camera or over the privacy
page. Cover: hosted in Germany; data stays in the EU; read-only scope; no
tracking cookies or ad pixels; who Parsius is. Short, unglamorous, and the most
likely video to get shared in a DACH merchant group.

### Video 4 — "Free WooCommerce low-stock plugin" (~60s)
*Publish when the plugin is live on WordPress.org.*

Install from the WP plugin directory → Steadel menu → the low-stock table, all
without an account. Then: *"If you'd rather be told than have to check, connect
it to Steadel in one click."*

## 5. Communities (C3)

Where: r/woocommerce, r/shopify, r/ecommerce, Indie Hackers, and DACH e-commerce
groups (Slack/Discord/Facebook) — the last group is the priority given DACH-first.

**How, in one rule:** answer the stock question as if Steadel didn't exist. Give
the manual method, the plugin, the competitor — whatever actually solves it. Only
mention Steadel when it's the direct answer, and say you built it.

**Reusable disclosure line:**

> Full disclosure, I build one of these (Steadel) — but the manual approach above
> works fine if you only have a handful of SKUs.

**Never:** drip-feed the same comment across subreddits, post a launch
announcement to a help thread, or use a second account. One flagged post costs
more than the channel returns.
